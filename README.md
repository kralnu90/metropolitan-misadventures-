# Metropolitan Misadventure in a Criminal City

A slapstick open-world crime and driving game for your phone. Nick cars, run jobs for five dodgy district contacts, build up safe houses and businesses, and take down Shifty Shaw.

**Two cities.** Kingsgate is home. Take the car ferry or the toll road to **Lumen City**, a neon sprawl of nightclubs, glass towers and arcades, with its own districts, jobs and a motel to crash in. Your car, cash and progress come with you.

**Lumen City's story.** Seven new contacts with five jobs each: Disco Dave, Pixel Pete, Captain Bev, Madame Zola, Lady Featherstonehaugh, Rupert Moneypenny-Crisp and Swifty. They unlock as you level up, and it all builds to a finale against Velvet Shaw, Shifty's posh big sister, with Del's Kingsgate crew coming over on the ferry to help.

**Daily courier run.** Swift & Shifty Couriers in Kingsgate have a parcel waiting in Lumen City every day. Collect it, bring it back, and get paid more for being quick and careful.

**Play it:** https://kralnugames.com/metropolitan-misadventures-/

Your progress saves in your browser on the device you play on.

## Updates

The game checks for a newer version when it opens and when you come back to it. If there is one, players see a small "A new version is ready" notice with an **Update** button, and there's a **Get the latest version** button in the menu at any time.

When pushing an update: change the game's version number and put the same value in `version.json`.
